Our story

From one small shop to a full online store

We started in 2022 with a single small shop in Lucknow. Back then, shopping meant visiting three different stores for three different products, waiting in queues and paying more than you had to. Store brings all of that into one place, at honest prices, straight to your account.

Today more than 12,500 customers trust us, and we have issued over 3,000 orders every week across twelve categories.
